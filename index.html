What We Do

Teach. Compete. Give back.

Run free coding classes

We design and teach camps for elementary, junior high, and local middle schoolers — plus international outreach.

  • Scratch, Python & ML for TKA Elementary
  • Free Python Winter Camp for TKA Junior High
  • Free Python Spring Camp for local middle schoolers
  • Free Python Camp for outreach to China
🧑‍💻

Compete in hackathons & contests

Our members represent The King's Academy at some of the Bay Area's biggest coding events.

  • Lockheed Martin Code Quest (Feb)
  • HPE CodeWars (Mar)
  • Los Altos Hacks (Apr)
  • …and more!

Our Partner

Voice of Calling.

Voice of Calling is a nonprofit organization where students can use their skills and passions to serve others. Our coding team partners with Voice of Calling by offering free online classes, creating educational resources, and volunteering to support younger students in learning technology.

It's a way for us to apply what we know, give back to the community, and grow as leaders. This year, the $2,000 we raised was used to help build a computer lab in Tanzania — providing internet access and computer science education to dozens of current and future high school and college students.
