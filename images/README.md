# Photos & logo

Drop your images into this folder using the **exact filenames** below and they'll
appear automatically on the site. Until a file exists, a labelled placeholder box
shows in its place (nothing breaks). JPG or PNG both work — just keep the name.

Recommended: landscape photos ~1600px wide; square headshots ~800×800px.

## Site icon (favicon)
| Filename | What it is |
|---|---|
| `logo.png` | Your team logo — used as the browser tab / favicon icon. A square PNG (512×512 recommended) works best. |
